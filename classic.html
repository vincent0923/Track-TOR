  • 估算总消耗 = 基础代谢 + 手表热量 + 消化耗能。消化耗能按营养素算：蛋白质热量 × 25%，碳水 × 8%，脂肪 × 2%；没记营养素的日子按摄入 × 9%。手填了当日总消耗，就用手填的。Est. burn = BMR + watch calories + digestion. Digestion is worked out per nutrient: 25% of protein calories, 8% of carbs, 2% of fat; days without nutrients use 9% of intake. A total you enter by hand replaces it.
  • 手表热量（高驰的活动热量，本来就不含基础代谢）：日常活动全算，但最多按步数估算值的 1.5 倍再加 120 kcal（手表整天心率偏高时会多估好几倍）；运动记录 × 0.7（心率估算容易偏高）；健走、家务等自定义活动、瑜伽这类低强度运动 × 0.5（心率只比静息高一点时最不准）；有功率计的骑行全算（功率是实测的，不打折）。当天要到晚上才完整。在「设定 › 图表和同步设定」可以改成全算。Watch calories (COROS active calories, which already leave out resting metabolism): everyday movement in full, up to 1.5 times what the steps account for plus 120 kcal (a watch that reads a raised heart rate all day can report several times more); logged workouts × 0.7 (heart-rate estimates run high); low-intensity ones such as walks, housework and other custom activities, and yoga × 0.5 (least accurate when heart rate is barely above resting); rides with a power meter in full (measured, not discounted). Today is only complete in the evening. Settings › Charts & sync can count them in full instead.
  • 脂肪对账：统计 14 天以上时，把估算的减脂和同期 7 日均体重的变化对一下：差在 15% 以内写「约」；估算比体重多掉 15% 以上写「激进估计」；少 15% 以上写「保守估计」。体重里有水分和肠道内容物，只能当参考；长按这块面板可以切换手表热量打折或全算。Fat cross-check: over 14 days or more, the estimated fat loss is compared with what the 7-day average weight did over the same days: within 15% reads "about", more than 15% above reads "aggressive estimate", more than 15% below reads "conservative estimate". Weight includes water and gut contents, so treat it as a guide; hold this tile to switch watch calories between discounted and in full.
  • 基础代谢：按 Mifflin 公式，用你的性别、年龄、身高和体重算。BMR: the Mifflin formula, from your sex, age, height and weight.
  • 摄入：当天记下的饮食热量合计。Intake: the calories you logged that day.
  • 缺口 / 盈余 = 估算总消耗 − 摄入。约 7700 kcal 折合 1 kg 脂肪。Deficit / surplus = est. burn − intake. About 7700 kcal makes 1 kg of fat.
  • 蛋白质（右边的轴，单位 g）：当天记下的蛋白质合计，画成半透明的粗线，不挡热量那两条线。粗线落在浅色带里或上面就是吃够了，在带子下面是没吃够。Protein (right axis, grams): the protein you logged that day, drawn as a broad see-through stroke so it doesn't hide the calorie lines. On or above the shaded band means enough; below it means short.
  • 增肌够用区间 = 体重 × 1.6 到 2.2 g。到下沿就够增肌了，再往上多长肌肉的效果很小。超过上沿也没问题：减脂期间多吃蛋白质更顶饱，消化耗能也高，还更能保住肌肉。Muscle-building band = body weight × 1.6 to 2.2 g. The bottom is enough for building muscle; above it, extra muscle gains are small. Going over the top is fine: while cutting, more protein keeps you fuller, costs more to digest and protects muscle.

进阶规则

  • 每 5 次加一档：D1–5、D6–10、D11–15、D16–20。
  • 某一项没做满，下次这一项沿用原来的数，做满再加。
  • 所有按次数的动作每组 5–12 个，不超过 12。要加量就加组，不加次数。
  • 标准俯卧撑：稳、慢，3-1-1，余 1–2 个或姿势变形就停，记录实际个数。所有组都到 12 个且留有余量，换更难的形式（底部停 2 秒）。
  • 标准引体：拉练第一个做，趁精力最好。每组留 1 个余力，组间休息 2–3 分钟；只算下巴过杠、手臂伸直的完整次数，做不满就记实际个数。
  • 从推练 D5 起取消下斜俯卧撑（出差、换场地无法统一高度），钻石俯卧撑改为 3 组；推拉日都加靠墙静蹲 3 × 20 秒。
  • 手腕有压力时钻石俯卧撑改握拳或用支架做。
  • 同肌群间隔 ≥48 小时；推日最多 1 个引体或不做。
  • 引体测试固定条件：精力充沛的日子、训练前、同一根杠、只做一组。

依据

  • 渐进超负荷：逐步加量是力量和肌肉增长的基本前提（ACSM 2009 进阶模型立场声明）。每 5 次加一档是为了好执行定的节奏，没有研究规定这个间隔。
  • 没做满就沿用原数：属于按表现自我调节，是教练实践中的常用做法，研究证据有限。
  • 每组 5–12 个、加组不加次数：只要接近力竭，从 6 次到 30 次以上的范围增肌效果相近（Schoenfeld 2017 荟萃分析）；每周组数越多，肌肉增长越多（Schoenfeld 2017 剂量反应研究）。所以加组是有效的加量方式。上限 12 是为了保持动作质量，不是生理界限。
  • 留 1–2 个余力：离力竭 0–3 个以内的训练增肌效果都好，越接近力竭增肌越多，对力量的影响则不大（Refalo 2023、Robinson 2024 荟萃分析）。留余力换来的是恢复和动作质量。
  • 慢速 3-1-1：每次动作 0.5–8 秒，增肌效果相近（Schoenfeld 2015 荟萃分析）。慢速的价值在于控制动作、让自重更难，不在于速度本身；单次超过 10 秒反而不利。
  • 组间休息 2–3 分钟：较长的组间休息比 1 分钟更利于力量和肌肉（Schoenfeld 2016）。
  • 离心引体：离心阶段能承受更大负荷，是还做不了完整引体时的常用过渡。离心训练提升力量有证据支持，专门针对引体的研究较少。
  • 同肌群间隔 ≥48 小时：来自通行的新手指南；每个肌群每周练 2 次，增肌效果好于 1 次（Schoenfeld 2016 频率荟萃分析）。
  • 休息日少量引体：即“润滑沟槽”（greasing the groove），来自实践经验，缺乏对照研究。
  • 平板支撑加到 75 秒：时长超过约 60 秒后收益递减。McGill 建议用多组 10 秒左右的短时支撑练核心耐力，可作为以后的替代。

骑行日

  • 先确认天气能骑，再按碳水日吃（150–200g）。不确定能不能骑，早餐按中档，多出的碳水留到骑完。
  • 进食窗口：出发前 2–3 小时正餐，或出发前 15 分钟内小份；饭后 30–90 分钟不出发。
  • 中间档那天：平均心率 <135，40–60 分钟，轻齿比，陡坡推车；到 140 就放慢。
  • 带水，超过 90 分钟带补给。骑完一餐配 ≥25g 低脂蛋白。

休息日

  • 不加练。标准引体 1–2 个，1–3 次，间隔几小时，永远留余力。
  • 碳水 50–100g；蛋白 114–120g，优先酸奶、FitMilk、鸡胸、虾。
  • 碳水不空腹单吃，同餐配 ≥20g 蛋白，主食最后吃。
  • 蔬菜 ≥300g，根茎类最多算 150g。
  • 每顿 8.5 分饱，先盛好再开吃。

Progression rules

  • Step up every 5 sessions: D1–5, D6–10, D11–15, D16–20.
  • If you miss the target on an exercise, keep the same number next time; increase only once you hit it.
  • Every rep-based exercise: 5–12 reps per set, never above 12. Add sets, not reps, to progress.
  • Standard push-ups: steady and slow, 3-1-1, stop 1–2 reps short or when form breaks, log actual reps. When every set reaches 12 with reps in reserve, switch to a harder variation (2 s pause at the bottom).
  • Strict pull-ups: first exercise of the pull day, while fresh. Leave 1 rep in reserve, rest 2–3 min between sets; count only full reps (chin over bar, arms straight) and log what you actually did.
  • From push D5 on: no decline push-ups (no consistent bench height when travelling or changing parks), diamond push-ups go to 3 sets, and both push and pull days add a 3 × 20 s wall sit.
  • If the wrists feel strained, do diamond push-ups on fists or with push-up handles.
  • At least 48 h between sessions for the same muscles; on push days at most 1 pull-up, or none.
  • Pull-up test, fixed conditions: a well-rested day, before training, same bar, one set only.

Evidence

  • Progressive overload: gradually adding load is the basic condition for strength and muscle gain (ACSM 2009 position stand on progression models). Stepping up every 5 sessions is a practical rhythm; no study prescribes that interval.
  • Repeat the number after a miss: performance-based autoregulation, common coaching practice with limited research behind it.
  • 5–12 reps per set, add sets not reps: taken close to failure, anywhere from about 6 to 30+ reps builds muscle similarly (Schoenfeld 2017 meta-analysis); more weekly sets bring more growth (Schoenfeld 2017 dose-response). So adding sets is a valid way to progress. The cap of 12 protects form; it is not a physiological limit.
  • 1–2 reps in reserve: sets ending 0–3 reps short of failure all build muscle well, with more growth closer to failure and little difference for strength (Refalo 2023, Robinson 2024 meta-analyses). Reserve buys recovery and form.
  • Slow 3-1-1 tempo: reps lasting 0.5–8 s give similar hypertrophy (Schoenfeld 2015 meta-analysis). Slow reps help control and make bodyweight harder; speed itself isn't the point, and reps over 10 s are worse.
  • 2–3 min rest between sets: longer rest beats 1 min for strength and size (Schoenfeld 2016).
  • Negative pull-ups: the lowering phase handles more load, a common bridge before full pull-ups. Eccentric training is supported for strength; pull-up-specific research is thin.
  • ≥48 h between same-muscle sessions: standard beginner guidance; training each muscle twice a week beats once (Schoenfeld 2016 frequency meta-analysis).
  • A few pull-ups on rest days: "greasing the groove", from practice rather than controlled studies.
  • Plank up to 75 s: returns diminish past about 60 s. McGill recommends repeated holds of around 10 s for core endurance, a possible later swap.

Riding days

  • Confirm the weather first, then eat as a carb day (150–200 g). If unsure, eat a medium-carb breakfast and save the extra carbs for after the ride.
  • Eating window: a full meal 2–3 h before, or a small snack within 15 min of leaving; don't set off 30–90 min after eating.
  • Middle-tier days: average HR <135, 40–60 min, easy gears, walk steep climbs; ease off at 140.
  • Take water; bring food for rides over 90 min. The meal after the ride gets ≥25 g of low-fat protein.

Rest days

  • No extra training. 1–2 strict pull-ups, 1–3 times, hours apart, always leaving reps in reserve.
  • Carbs 50–100 g; protein 114–120 g, prioritising yogurt, FitMilk, chicken breast, prawns.
  • Never eat carbs alone on an empty stomach: pair with ≥20 g protein, eat the starch last.
  • Vegetables ≥300 g, counting at most 150 g of root vegetables.
  • Eat each meal to 8.5/10 full; plate it before you start.
