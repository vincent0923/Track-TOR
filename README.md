# 推拉机 track-TOR

**骑行佬练就薄肌上身的爽感健身记录 App。**

腿和心肺交给骑车，上半身交给推拉机。每练完一组就放一朵烟花，把记录训练变成打游戏通关。

👉 **打开就用：https://vincent0923.github.io/Track-TOR/**

不用安装，不用注册，数据只存在你自己的手机里。

## 为什么做它

骑车把腿和心肺练得很好，上半身却常年闲置。可上身力量对控车、摇车、爬坡和长时间低趴都有帮助，更重要的是，穿骑行服好看。

很多人不缺训练计划，缺的是坚持。所以推拉机把重点放在“爽”上：练完就有反馈，进步看得见。

## 能做什么

| 功能 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 🏋️ 推拉训练 | 推日、拉日交替。训练引擎看你前 3 次的表现排下 2 次：做到 10 个就换更难的动作或慢速，达标就加次数，连着没达标就减，每次 5–12 组，每项都写为什么 |
| 💪 随时加练 | 每个训练日都能加练，默认引体向上，也能加自定义项目 |
| 🎆 爽感反馈 | 专注训练模式：一次一个动作，拇指位大勾，组间自动倒计时；每组有音效和震动，做完一个动作撒彩纸，整天练完全屏庆祝 |
| 📈 看得见的进步 | 目标进度环，引体、体重、腰围、睡眠、HRV、静息心率的趋势卡片，点开看完整曲线、记一笔 |
| 🪙 G | 力量、减脂、腰围有真实进步就拿 G，以后在主题商店换主题 |
| ⌚ 连接高驰 | 授权一次，自动续期，同步健康和活动数据 |
| 🍽️ 饮食账本 | 在饮食页填总热量和蛋白质、脂肪、碳水、蔬菜，也能逐项记明细，自动算热量缺口和约合多少脂肪；也能粘贴任何在线表格的分享链接（Google 表格、腾讯文档或自己的网址），拉取每日饮食 |
| 💾 进度文件 | 一键存成 txt 放在手机里，换手机选这个文件就能恢复 |
| 📲 装到主屏 | 像 App 一样全屏运行，没网也能记 |

## 开始使用

1. 打开 [网页](https://vincent0923.github.io/Track-TOR/)，想要的话装到手机主屏：
   - Android（Chrome）：菜单 →「安装应用」
   - iPhone（Safari）：分享 →「添加到主屏幕」
2. 第一次打开时填性别、出生年月、身高、体重和目标，都可以留空，以后点右上角头像改。
3. 有高驰手表的话，点右上角头像 →「备份进度与高驰」→「连接高驰」，选账号所在区域，登录授权一次。授权后会自动同步，并填好性别、出生年月和身高。授权不成功就换个区域再试。不用高驰也能记训练和饮食。

想用原来的界面和 29 套主题：右上角头像 →「经典界面」；在经典界面的「设定 › 主题」里可以换回来。

## 你的数据

- 所有记录都存在你手机的浏览器里，没有账号，也没有我们的服务器。
- 高驰由你的浏览器直接连接，不经过第三方。
- 浏览器偶尔会清空本地数据，记得定期点备份图标保存进度文件。
- 进度文件带着你的高驰授权，换手机恢复后不用重新授权，所以不要把它发给别人。
- 想断开高驰，在「备份进度」里登出，可以选择保留数据，或只清除高驰同步来的数据。

# track-TOR

**The feel-good workout log that builds upper-body muscle for cyclists.**

Cycling already keeps you lean and takes care of your legs and lungs. track-TOR builds the upper body it leaves behind. Every set you finish gets its reward, so logging a workout feels like clearing a game level.

👉 **Open it now: https://vincent0923.github.io/Track-TOR/**

No install, no sign-up, and your data stays on your own phone.

## Why it exists

Cycling builds great legs and cardio while the upper body sits idle. Yet upper-body strength helps with bike control, sprinting, climbing, and holding a low position. And it looks better in a jersey.

Most people don't need another training plan. They need a reason to keep going. So track-TOR is built around how good it feels: instant rewards, and progress you can see.

## What it does

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| 🏋️ Push and pull training | Push and pull days take turns. The training engine looks at your last 3 sessions to plan the next 2: reach 10 reps and the move gets harder or slower, hit the target and reps go up, miss twice and it eases off; 5–12 sets a session, each with its reason |
| 💪 Extra practice | Add extra sets to any session, pull-ups by default or any exercise you like |
| 🎆 Feel-good rewards | A focus mode for training: one exercise at a time, a thumb-sized tick, automatic rest countdowns; sound and a buzz for every set, confetti for every finished exercise, a full-screen celebration for the day |
| 📈 Visible progress | Goal rings and trend cards for pull-ups, weight, waist, sleep, HRV, and resting heart rate; open one for the full chart or to log a value |
| 🪙 G | Real progress in strength, fat loss and waist earns G (for Grind), to spend on themes in the shop later |
| ⌚ COROS connection | Authorize once and it renews itself, syncing your health and activity data |
| 🍽️ Diet ledger | On the Diet tab, enter total calories plus protein, fat, carbs, and veg, or log item by item and see your calorie gap and the fat it adds up to. It can also pull your daily food from any online table by pasting its link (Google Sheets, Tencent Docs, or your own URL) |
| 💾 Progress file | Save a txt file to your phone, then pick it to restore on a new one |
| 📲 Home screen install | Runs full screen like an app and works offline |

## Get started

1. Open the [web app](https://vincent0923.github.io/Track-TOR/). You can also add it to your home screen:
   - Android (Chrome): Menu → Install app
   - iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen
2. On first open, fill in your sex, birth month, height, weight, and goals. Any of them can stay blank; change them later from the avatar at the top right.
3. If you have a COROS watch, tap the avatar → Back up & COROS → **Connect COROS**, choose your account region, and authorize once. It syncs right away and fills in your sex, birth month, and height. If authorization fails, try another region. Training and diet logging work without COROS.

Switch the language under the avatar › Language. The classic interface and its 29 themes are under the avatar › Classic interface.

## Your data

- Everything stays in your phone's browser. There are no accounts and no servers of ours.
- Your browser connects to COROS directly, with no third party in between.
- Browsers sometimes clear local data, so tap the backup icon now and then to save a progress file.
- The progress file includes your COROS authorization, so restoring on a new phone needs no new sign-in. Don't send it to other people.
- To disconnect COROS, log out from the Back up panel. You can keep your data or delete only what COROS synced.
